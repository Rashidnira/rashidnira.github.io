About Me

I am Mohotarema Rashid, originally from Bangladesh. I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Science at the University of North Texas, specializing in Data Science. I expect to graduate in Fall 2026.

My research uses large language models (LLMs), natural language processing (NLP), and behavioral theory to study financial misinformation and investor behavior. My background is in finance, and I previously worked as an Assistant Professor of Finance. I bring strong computational skills to interdisciplinary research connecting finance, AI, and social science.

I now teach data visualization at UNT using Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and Tableau.

I am seeking remote postdoctoral positions starting after graduation and research consulting roles. In these roles, I aim to apply my quantitative background to analyze large, heterogeneous datasets and turn findings into actionable insights.

Research Projects

FinBench — Coverage-Stratified Benchmark for LLM Trading Agents

Data and Information Management, forthcoming · code and data public

A benchmark built on the observation that LLM trading agents are almost always evaluated on the same heavily-covered mega-caps. FinBench stratifies by analyst coverage instead: sixteen U.S. equities — six highly covered mega-caps and ten under-covered firms — observed daily from 2023 to 2025. Each firm-trading-day encodes ten heterogeneous sources as decision-ready signals, with news, social media, and SEC filings summarized and sentiment-scored by Qwen2.5-14B, and structured sources reduced deterministically to interpretable state labels.

github.com/Rashidnira/coverage-stratified-llm-trading

Fin-Debate — Multi-Agent Fact-Checking for Financial Claims

Information Processing & Management, forthcoming

A single LLM pass on a financial claim tends to hedge toward "half-true." Fin-Debate instead runs three analysts with opposing briefs — supportive, contradictory, and critical — independently, so none anchors on another, then has a judge synthesize their disagreement into a verdict. An iterative retrieval pipeline validates temporal alignment of evidence before any of it reaches the analysts.

300 PolitiFact financial claims (2007–2025) · ~63% macro F1 · ≈10 points over baseline

Fin_Agent — Multilingual Financial NLP at FinEval 2026

FinNLP 2026 shared task, three subtasks · code public

Task-aware systems rather than one general model: annotation-aware numeric rules combined with zero-shot Qwen2.5-32B classification for Greek financial NER; an interpretable cue-based ordinal scorer for Japanese implicit communication, which needs no LLM inference at all; and BGE-M3 retrieval with Qwen3-32B generation for multilingual financial QA over SEC filings and contemporaneous news in five languages.

Greek NER · 0.9521 public F1, 4th  |  Japanese ICR · 98.0%  |  Multilingual QA · 0.3419 ROUGE-1

github.com/Rashidnira/finagent-fineval-2026
